# Ranger Burgers Restaurant Knowledge Base

> Version: 2026 Edition, Store #218, Gilbert, Arizona

- **Brand Name**: Ranger Burgers
- **Outlet Type**: Quick Service Restaurant (QSR) with a dining room and a drive-thru
- **Address**: Store #218, Gilbert, Arizona
- **Cuisine**: classic American fast food
- **Hours**: Dining room 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily · Drive-thru 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM Sunday through Thursday · Drive-thru 6:00 AM to 12:00 AM (midnight) Friday and Saturday
- **Breakfast Hours**: 6:00 AM to 10:30 AM daily | **Lunch and Dinner Menu**: 10:30 AM until close
- **Last Order**: The kitchen stops taking orders 10 minutes before the posted closing time
- **Restrooms**: Inside the dining room, open to all guests during lobby hours; no code or purchase needed
- **Payment**: Credit cards, debit cards, mobile wallets, and cash; no personal checks
- **Loyalty Program**: Ranger Rewards app, free to join, 10 points per dollar spent, redeemable for free menu items
- **Catering and Large Group Orders**: At least 24 hours notice; call Store #218 ahead of time

## Dietary and Allergen Notes
- **Vegetarian items**: Garden Side Salad, Ranger Fries, Ranger Onion Rings, Hash Rounds, Pancake Stack, Egg and Cheese Biscuit, Ranger Fried Apple Pie, Peach Cobbler Hand Pie, Soft-Serve Cone, Caramel Apple Sundae, and all four milkshake flavors (Vanilla, Chocolate, Strawberry, Banana Split). Vegetarian items contain no meat, poultry, or fish, but may contain dairy or egg.
- **No veggie burger**: There's no meatless burger patty on the menu. Every burger has a beef patty and every chicken sandwich has chicken.
- **Not vegetarian**: Loaded Ranger Fries (bacon bits), Ranger Sunrise Biscuit (sausage), every burger, chicken sandwich, nugget, and meal.
- **Dairy-free options**: Ranger Fried Apple Pie, Ranger Fries, Hash Rounds, the Garden Side Salad without cheese, fountain drinks, iced tea, black coffee, and bottled water. The shakes, Soft-Serve Cone, sundae, and cheese toppings all contain dairy.
- **Spicy items**: Blaze Chicken Sandwich, Fire Ranger Double, and Spicy Ranger Nuggets. The Blaze Chicken Sandwich is the spiciest item on the menu. The Sonoran Green Chile Burger has a mild, tangy heat.
- **Allergens**: Buns and breading contain gluten, wheat, and soy. Ranger Burgers uses a shared kitchen and fryer, so items may come into contact with gluten, dairy, peanut, soy, and egg. Ask the counter staff for a full ingredient and allergen list for any item.

## Policies
- **Substitutions**: Burgers and chicken sandwiches come with all toppings; ask to hold anything at no extra charge. Value menu items are served exactly as listed, with no substitutions.
- **Wrong or missing items**: Ranger Burgers remakes a wrong order free of charge. If a remake isn't wanted, an incorrect order is refunded to the original payment method with a receipt.
- **Kids meal toy**: The Ranger Rustlers Kids Meal includes a small toy while supplies last; toys rotate weekly and can't be swapped for an item or a refund.
- **Outside food**: Outside food and drinks aren't allowed in the dining room.
- **Lost and found**: Kept at the counter for 7 days.
- **Holiday hours**: Closed Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. Closes at 6:00 PM on Christmas Eve. Open 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on New Year's Day.
- **Order ahead**: Through the Ranger Burgers app or website, for pickup at the counter or the mobile order spot.
- **Refills**: Free fountain drink and iced tea refills in the dining room only.
- **Drive-thru**: The drive-thru stays open later than the dining room. Drive-thru and to-go orders don't include free refills.
